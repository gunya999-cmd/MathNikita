## Source anchors, 2014 numbering

### №1113
A shop sells potatoes over three days. Day 1 is 32% of the original amount. Day 2 is 45% of what remained after day 1. Day 3 is 561 kg.

Reasoning:
- after day 1: 68% remains;
- after day 2: 55% of that 68% remains;
- day 3 is therefore 0.68 × 0.55 = 0.374 = 37.4% of the original amount;
- original amount = 561 / 0.374 = 1500 kg.

Anchor answer: **1500 kg**.

### №1115
Roses are 60% of all flowers. Gladioli are 40% of the number of roses. There are 32 dahlias. Find the number of roses.

Reasoning:
- gladioli = 40% of 60% = 24% of all flowers;
- roses + gladioli = 84%, so dahlias = 16%;
- 16% = 32 flowers, so all flowers = 200;
- roses = 60% of 200 = 120.

Anchor answer: **120 roses**.

### №1120
Two motorboats leave two piers 63 km apart toward each other. One boat travels at 16 km/h. They meet after 2 h 6 min. Find the second speed.

Reasoning:
- 2 h 6 min = 2.1 h;
- combined speed = 63 / 2.1 = 30 km/h;
- second speed = 30 − 16 = 14 km/h.

Anchor answer: **14 km/h**.

## Pedagogical role

Lesson 153 closes §38. It should not merely repeat the one-step inverse-percent rule. The lesson combines:
- percent of a remainder;
- changing bases in successive percentages;
- reconstructing a whole from a derived percentage;
- translating a verbal relation into a percent of the whole;
- unit conversion inside a composite problem;
- reverse checking.
