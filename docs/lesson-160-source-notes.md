## Source fidelity policy

The KTP verifies topic, paragraph and exercise numbers, but the exact wording of those textbook exercises was not independently verified from a primary textbook scan. Therefore the lesson uses those numbers only as **adapted anchors**. No task in lesson 160 is marked `sourceExact: true`.

## Pedagogical scope

The lesson reviews:

1. decimal place alignment;
2. appending zeros to the right of the fractional part;
3. exact addition and subtraction;
4. multi-step expressions and parentheses;
5. equations involving decimal addition/subtraction;
6. applied problems with money, distance, mass and volume;
7. inverse-operation checking.

## Assessment contract

- 6 concept stages;
- 20 practice tasks;
- exactly 50 checked response fields;
- 1 summary stage;
- 27 stages total;
- 50-minute planning duration;
- strict decimal equality without floating-point tolerance;
- comma and dot are equivalent decimal separators;
- trailing zeros are ignored (`12,70 = 12.7`);
- fraction syntax such as `1/2` is intentionally rejected in this decimal-focused lesson;
- progress is persisted under `mathnikita-lesson-160-progress-v1`.
