# Lesson 169 source notes

## KTP placement

The 175-hour Merzlyak grade-5 calendar plan used by MathNikita places lesson 169 in the final review block:

- 169 — «Решение текстовых задач»;
- 170 — «Решение текстовых задач»;
- 171 — «Решение текстовых задач»;
- 172 — итоговая контрольная работа №10.

The same row attaches `§24 №1192` to lesson 169. That reference is not treated as a reliable content anchor: §24 belongs to the combinatorics block and continues the shifted-homework pattern already reconciled in lessons 165–168. The lesson topic itself — «Решение текстовых задач» — is therefore the authoritative KTP signal for lesson 169.

Source checked: 175-hour working programme / KTP for Merzlyak grade 5 (Znanio, Антонова Н. В.).

## Scope decision

Lesson 169 is the first of three consecutive text-problem review lessons. It focuses on building a mathematical model from prose rather than on one narrow textbook paragraph:

- identifying known and unknown quantities;
- selecting operations or a simple equation;
- matching units before calculation;
- uniform motion, meeting and catch-up;
- price × quantity and unit-price models;
- fractions and percentages in context;
- perimeter, area and volume in context;
- arithmetic mean;
- checking the result against the original question.

Lessons 170–171 can increase multi-step difficulty and use the later KTP review anchors without duplicating lesson 169.

## Provenance policy

All 20 lesson-169 tasks are authored/adapted final-review problems. No task claims verbatim wording from Merzlyak and every task has `sourceExact:false`. The inconsistent `§24 №1192` reference is documented here but is deliberately not presented to students as the source of a text-problem exercise.
