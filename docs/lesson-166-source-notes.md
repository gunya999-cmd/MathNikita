## Pedagogical scope

The lesson reviews:

1. acute, right, obtuse and straight angles;
2. degree measure and protractor reading;
3. addition and subtraction of adjacent angles;
4. 90° and 180° as reference totals;
5. angle bisectors;
6. reverse problems for unknown angle parts;
7. checking a constructed angle by re-measuring it.
