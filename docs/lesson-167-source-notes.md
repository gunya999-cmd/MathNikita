## Source reconciliation

The published 175-hour KTP attaches `§11,12 №1189` to lesson 167 even though §11–12 and exercises №1189, №1191, №1192 belong to the angle-review material used in lesson 166. This is the same shifted-reference pattern already documented for lessons 165–166.

Independent KTP variants consistently identify **§24** as `Комбинаторные задачи`. A 169/170-hour sequence places the combinatorial review at §24 and the angle review immediately before it at §§11–12.

Therefore lesson 167 follows the verified topic **«Комбинаторные задачи»** and textbook **§24**. The incorrect shifted reference `§11,12 №1189` is not presented to students as a combinatorics source.

Exact wording of a primary textbook end-of-course exercise for this KTP row was not verified. All practice is therefore authored/adapted and no task is marked `sourceExact: true`.

## Pedagogical scope

The lesson reviews:

1. systematic exhaustive enumeration;
2. tree diagrams and tables;
3. multiplication principle;
4. addition principle for disjoint cases;
5. ordered versus unordered choices;
6. choices with and without repetition;
7. leading-zero and parity restrictions;
8. required and forbidden cases;
9. checking completeness and avoiding double counting.
