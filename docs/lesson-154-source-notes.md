## Pedagogic role
Lesson 154 is a diagnostic/systematization lesson, not a continuation of §38:
1. arithmetic mean and recovering a missing value from a known mean;
2. finding a percent of a known whole;
3. finding a whole from a known percentage part;
4. complement to 100%;
5. changing percent base in compound tasks;
6. mixed mean→percent and percent→whole chains.
