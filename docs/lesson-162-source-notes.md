The KTP verifies the topic, paragraphs and exercise numbers. The exact wording of exercises №1177, №1182 and №1203 was not independently verified from a primary textbook scan, so lesson 162 uses those numbers only as **adapted anchors**. No lesson-162 task is marked `sourceExact: true`.

## Pedagogical scope

The lesson reviews:

1. converting percentages to decimal coefficients;
2. finding p% of a number;
3. reconstructing 100% from a known p% part;
4. discounts and markups;
5. percentages below 1% and above 100%;
6. reverse checking;
7. identifying the correct 100% base in multistep tasks.
