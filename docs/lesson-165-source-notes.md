## Important source inconsistency

The published KTP row for lesson 165 attaches `§13, 14 №1193, 1194, 1197` to the topic «Площади и объёмы». This conflicts with the same program's earlier mapping of the textbook:

- §13 — «Многоугольники. Равные фигуры»;
- §14 — «Треугольник и его виды»;
- §21 — «Площадь. Площадь прямоугольника»;
- §22 — «Прямоугольный параллелепипед. Пирамида»;
- §23 — «Объём прямоугольного параллелепипеда».

The following KTP row (lesson 166) contains `§21-23 №1199, 1200`, even though its topic is angles. Independent exercise lookups confirm that №1199 and №1200 are volume tasks (cube scaling and halving all dimensions of a rectangular parallelepiped). This strongly indicates a one-row shift in the homework/reference column around lessons 164–167.

Therefore lesson 165 follows the verified **topic** «Площади и объёмы» and the internally consistent textbook sections **§§21–23**. №1199 and №1200 are used only as adapted exercise anchors. The inconsistent row references №1193, №1194 and №1197 are documented here but are not presented to students as area/volume source tasks.

No lesson-165 task is marked `sourceExact: true` because exact wording was not verified from a primary textbook scan.

## Pedagogical scope

The lesson reviews:

1. area of a rectangle and square;
2. reverse area problems;
3. square-unit conversion;
4. rectangular parallelepiped and cube;
5. volume formulas `V=abc` and `V=S_base·h`;
6. reverse volume problems;
7. cubic-unit conversion;
8. surface area as a sum of face areas;
9. scaling effects on area and volume.
