## Pedagogical scope

Lesson 162 covered the base direct/inverse percentage algorithms. Lesson 163 intentionally raises difficulty:

1. successive percentage changes;
2. products of percentage coefficients;
3. why equal percentage increase/decrease do not usually cancel;
4. changing the 100% base between steps;
5. percentages of subgroups;
6. reverse reconstruction after one or several changes;
7. percentages below 1% and above 100%;
8. full forward verification after reverse solving.
