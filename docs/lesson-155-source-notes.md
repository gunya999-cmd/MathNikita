## Pedagogical target

Lesson 154 rebuilt the conceptual map. Lesson 155 is a control-work rehearsal:
1. recognize the model without being told which section to use;
2. execute the calculation;
3. check the result by reverse calculation or reasonableness;
4. switch percentage base correctly in multistep problems;
5. combine arithmetic mean and percentages.

Coverage:
- arithmetic mean and recovering an unknown value from a known mean;
- direct percentage of a known whole;
- restoring 100% from a known percentage;
- percentages above 100%;
- loss/remainder and complement to 100%;
- changed percentage base;
- mixed mean + percent chains;
- short word problems similar in structure to the unit assessment.
