## Scope decision

Lesson 169 introduced translation from prose into one mathematical model. Lesson 170 raises the difficulty by requiring two or three dependent steps:

- routes split into multiple stages;
- delayed-start meeting and catch-up problems;
- percentage or fractional change followed by another operation;
- percentage of the remaining quantity rather than the original quantity;
- sequential price changes;
- reverse models such as restoring an original price after a discount;
- geometry embedded in a word problem;
- unit conversion inside a multi-step model;
- use of an intermediate result as input to the next step.

Lesson 171 is reserved for the final/hardest text-problem review and any later exercise anchors that can be verified safely, avoiding duplication with 169–170.
