## Source fidelity policy

The KTP verifies topic, paragraphs and exercise numbers, but the exact wording of those textbook exercises was not independently verified from a primary textbook scan. Therefore lesson 161 uses those numbers only as **adapted anchors**. No task is marked `sourceExact: true`.

## Pedagogical scope

The lesson reviews:

1. multiplication of decimal fractions and decimal-place counting;
2. multiplication/division by 10, 100 and 1000;
3. division of a decimal fraction by a natural number;
4. division by a decimal fraction via equal decimal shift;
5. inverse-operation checks;
6. equations with multiplication and division;
7. applied problems involving price, movement, area and productivity.
