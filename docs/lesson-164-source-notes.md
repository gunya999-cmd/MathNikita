## Pedagogical scope

The lesson reviews:

1. polygon sides and vertices;
2. perimeter as the length of a closed boundary;
3. summing all side lengths exactly once;
4. equal sides and regular polygons;
5. rectangles and squares as polygons;
6. finding an unknown side from a known perimeter;
7. decimal side lengths;
8. irregular and composite polygonal boundaries;
9. checking units and recomputing the full perimeter.
